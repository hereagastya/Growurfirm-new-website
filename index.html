What clients say.

Questions people ask us.

Something else on your mind? Ask us on WhatsApp.

How fast can you build my website?

Most websites go live in 3 to 5 days after we agree on the plan. If you need it sooner, express delivery is available for an extra charge.

Why a custom CRM instead of Zoho, HubSpot or Salesforce?

Off-the-shelf CRMs make you change your process to fit them, and charge per user every month. We set the CRM up around your stages, your team and your payment structure. You own it, and there are no per-seat fees that grow with your team.

Who owns what you build?

You do. The code, the data and the domain belong to your business. If you ever want to move to another team, we hand everything over.

What happens after launch?

We host, maintain and update what we build, and add features when you need them. Most clients stay with us for years, because it's easier than explaining their business to a new vendor.

Can the CRM work for my industry?

Very likely. The first version ran an education business, but the stages, fields and roles are all configurable. We've mapped it for real estate, clinics, service businesses, D2C brands and distributors. Tell us how you work and we'll show you how it would look.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work remotely and across time zones, and we're available every day from 9 am to 9 pm IST.

How do payments work?

You get a fixed quote first. Usually part is paid to start and the rest on launch. For CRMs and larger builds we split it into milestones.
