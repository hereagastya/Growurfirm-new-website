Website questions.

What do you need from me to start?

Your logo, a few photos if you have them, and a short call about your business. We can write the text for you, or polish what you have.

Do you handle the domain and hosting?

Yes. We can register the domain in your name and host the site for you, or use what you already have.

Can I update the website myself?

Business websites and stores come with a simple way to edit content, products and blog posts. For anything bigger, message us and we'll do it.

Will it show up on Google?

We set up everything Google needs to understand and list your site: titles, descriptions, structured data, a sitemap and speed. Ranking also depends on your competition and content, and we'll tell you honestly what to expect.

Which payment gateways can the store use?

Popular Indian gateways such as Razorpay, Cashfree and PhonePe, covering UPI, cards and net banking. International payments are possible too.
