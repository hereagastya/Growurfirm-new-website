What's inside.

9 stages from new enquiry to won or lost. Every lead gets a score and a source, so your team calls the hottest ones first and you know which ads actually work.

Facebook & Instagram leads, live

A live Meta Ads connection sends every ad lead straight into the CRM within seconds. Nobody downloads spreadsheets from the ads manager again.

Customer journey tracking

Follow each customer through as many stages as your business has. The first version runs a 22-stage student journey. Yours can be 8 or 30.

Tasks and follow-ups

Every lead and client has a next step with a date and an owner. Your team starts each morning with a list, and overdue items come to you.

Documents with versions

Team accounts and roles

CRM questions.

Usually a few weeks, depending on how many stages, roles and integrations you need. After the discovery call we give you a fixed timeline along with the quote.

How much does a custom CRM cost?

It depends on your scope, so we quote after a free call. Because the core is already built and proven, you pay for setting it up for your business, not for building a CRM from zero. There are no per-user fees.

Yes. We import your existing leads, customers and payment records, clean up duplicates, and check them with you before going live.

Yes. Your team can check follow-ups, update leads and upload documents from their phones.
